My Top Anime Watches

In this section I'm including a list of some of my favorite anime, Feel free to click on them for more details. I primarily use Crunchyroll to watch anime however this will probably change soon due to pricing updates :(

Descriptions and thoughts will be added for each soon!

BNA I loved this show! I think the art was amazing and the plot kept itself together while still allowing for off topic epsiodes. The wrap-up in the end of the story was very enjoyable and the show was great overall.
Evangelion This is probably one of the oldest anime I've watched but I can see why it was a classic. It had an interesting plot that honestly I couldn't follow at all times , especially the ending, but I think that those smarter than i could sum it up better.
The Emiscence in Shadow I like shows that have a concept like these where the main character generally minds there own buisness for the most point. The plot is a little hard to follow as it's mostly just actions sequences so I'd watch this one for the fights rather than the plot.
Frieren I liked this show a Lot! Especially because of the fantasy setting where it felt like medieval times but with magic in the world. I think the world building was really good and most of the characters had story built around them so it was nice to see the story switch perspectives.
One Punch Man I like this show as it's another show where the main character is laid back and just trying to live life but it's a world where life isn't easy to live. The fact that fights seem to get harder and harder for everyone else while the main character is just chilling is funny to me.
